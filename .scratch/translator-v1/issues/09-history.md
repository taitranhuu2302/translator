# 09: History

**What to build:** Everything the app translates, Replaces or Improves is kept locally and can be found again. Old entries age out on their own, so the list stays useful without anyone maintaining it, and nothing is ever sent anywhere.

**Blocked by:** 07

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Every translate, Replace and Improve is recorded locally with both sides, the provider, the mode and a timestamp
- [ ] History is searchable by either side of the Language Pair
- [ ] Entries older than 30 days are purged at launch and again daily
- [ ] History stays on this Mac; no history content is sent to any provider or sync service
- [ ] An entry can be reopened in the Translate Window with its text and direction restored
- [ ] With an empty history the surface shows an empty state rather than an error
